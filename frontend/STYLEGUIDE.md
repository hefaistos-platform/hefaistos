Theme: "Retro-Tech" / "Modern 8-bit". The platform is a "forge"; it should feel like a precise, custom-built tool.

Clarity over Clutter: The UI must be "clean and easy." We will use ample whitespace and clear hierarchies. Pixel art is an accent, not the entire UI.

Function-First: Design must enhance functionality, not hide it. Interactive elements must be obvious.

Eye-Friendly: The "blue" theme will be light and professional, not a dark "hacker" theme, to reduce eye strain during long analysis sessions.
